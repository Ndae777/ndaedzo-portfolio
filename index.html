About me

I'm a final-year BCom Information Systems student at the University of Johannesburg, graduating in December 2026. University gave me the foundation, but most of what I can build I picked up on my own through CS50, bootcamps and a lot of projects.

In 2026 that turned into two live web apps built in teams: the backend of Zennith, a clinic system for South African public clinics, and the ESWA Wellbeing Hub for a non-profit that supports teachers.

What I like about IS is that it sits between business and technology. Before I write code, I think about why something should exist and who it helps.
